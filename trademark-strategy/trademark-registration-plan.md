## 4. Application Process and Documentation

### Required Documentation

#### Basic Filing Requirements
1. **Trademark Application Forms**
2. **Specimen of Use** (for used marks)
3. **Intent to Use Declaration** (if not yet in commerce)
4. **Power of Attorney** (for attorney representation)
5. **Priority Documents** (for international filings)

#### Supporting Materials
- Logo artwork in required formats
- Detailed descriptions of goods/services
- Evidence of use in commerce
- Marketing materials showing trademark use
- Corporate documentation

### Filing Strategy Options

#### Option 1: Intent-to-Use (ITU)
- File before commercial use
- 6-month period to show use
- Extensions available
- Secures priority date early

#### Option 2: Use-Based Application
- File after commercial use begins
- Requires evidence of use
- Faster examination process
- Immediate protection upon registration

## 7. International Considerations

### Madrid Protocol Strategy

#### Advantages
- Single application for multiple countries
- Centralized management system
- Cost-effective for multiple jurisdictions
- Simplified renewal process

#### Designated Countries
- All EU member states
- Japan, South Korea, Singapore
- Switzerland, Norway, Iceland
- Australia, New Zealand

### Regional Variations

#### European Union Trademark (EUTM)
- Single filing covers 27 member countries
- Unitary rights across EU
- Cost-effective for European coverage
- Central attack risk (vulnerability)

#### United States Specific Requirements
- Specimen of use requirements
- Section 8 and 15 filings
- Renewal every 10 years
- Strong enforcement mechanisms
